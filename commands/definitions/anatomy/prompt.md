Size the figure and start it, before you write any geometry.

Work out which of the three sizes the reader's idea actually is, say so in one
line with a time estimate, and get a page opening in the browser within the
first hour rather than at the end.

- **Figure** — one mechanism, 40 to 80 parts, SVG with at most one WebGL layer.
  About 30 to 90 minutes. Build it without asking.
- **Hero** — a dense object, 80 to 150 parts, or one 3D subject. About two to
  four hours. Say so, then build it unless they asked for something quick.
- **Epic** — a whole vehicle, building or plant with many moving systems. Six
  hours or more. Do not start it. Give the estimate, propose a Hero that keeps
  the core of the idea, list what the full version would add, and build the Epic
  only when they choose it.

Then follow the `anatomy` skill from the top. Do not skip to the geometry: the
parts list and the choice of object decide the quality.

Stop and ask when the size does not match the request — an Epic named as though
it were a Figure, or a Figure asked for at Epic scale. That is a scope question,
not a judgement call to make silently.
