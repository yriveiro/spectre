- Verified at OpenCode tag `v2.3.0` (`packages/core/src/skill.ts:58-92`): when `path.basename(skill.path) === "SKILL.md"` (and not a `skill://` URI), `Skill.prepare` runs `fs.scan("**/*", { cwd: directory, absolute: true, include: "file", dot: true, ignore: FSUtil.ignoreGlobs })`, filters out `SKILL.md`, sorts, slices up to 10 files, and renders `<skill_files>` alongside `Base directory for this skill`.
- Folding sibling `references/*.md`, `examples/`, or `scripts/` files into one monolithic `SKILL.md` is unnecessary and wastes context tokens on every load.
- Skills like `effect-best-practices`, `elysia-best-practices`, `solidjs-best-practices`, and `model-router` keep concise `SKILL.md` routers and load specific sibling files on demand.
