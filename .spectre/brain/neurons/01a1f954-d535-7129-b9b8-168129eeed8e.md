- `tools/definitions/nocturna/ledger.ts` persists per-project audited session `updatedAt` watermarks in `ctx.storage` behind a single-permit `Effect.Semaphore` (pruning entries older than 90 days or past 5,000 rows) so unchanged sessions are skipped until resumed without dirtying git.
- `isExcludedSession` skips `agent === "nocturna"`, the caller session, and direct children of the caller to prevent self-audit feedback loops.
