- `landing()` in `tools/definitions/worktrees/survey.ts` runs `git merge-base --is-ancestor` then `git cherry origin/main <branch>`; per-commit patch-ids match 1-commit squashes but report `patches: "unlanded"` on multi-commit squash PRs (`ses_e0d2f2968ffeDlSj0uBVSfy304`: 6 merged worktrees holding 2.0 GB).
- When triaging `review` rows whose `pr` is `#N/MERGED`, verify `git diff origin/main...<branch>` is empty rather than relying on `git log --oneline main..<branch>`, which always lists the pre-squash commits.
- `tools.spectre.goal` (`arm`) writes `.spectre/run.md` untracked (`dirty: "scratch:1"`), which passes `safe` bucketing once merged but causes `ctx.worktree.remove(..., force: false)` to fail with `forceRequired` (`ses_e0b1a25b9ffeJ1LMCggLSynElV`, `ses_e86ed4b68ffe8Swy12l1O8ZaD9`) until `.spectre/run.md` is inspected and removed.
