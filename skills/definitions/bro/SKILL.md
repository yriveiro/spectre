# Bro

Restate your last message in plain words, the way one person explains something to
another. Output only the restatement.

- Drop the term, not the fact. "The effect fails the context window" becomes
  "running out of memory makes the effect fail". "Idempotent" becomes "running it
  twice does the same thing as running it once". A caveat is information and a term
  is a label, so the caveat stays and the term goes.
- Read the two texts side by side. That is the check: name one word the reader
  would have to look up. If you can name it, it survived the rewrite.
- Say it out loud in your head. A sentence you would not say to the person in front
  of you is still written for somebody who is not in the room.

Never trade a caveat for a shorter sentence. A lost caveat is a wrong answer, not a
hard one.
